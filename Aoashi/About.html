Story and Plot:

The story follows Ashito Aoi, a young football player from Ehime, Japan.

Ashito is talented, aggressive and passionate about football. He dreams of becoming a professional footballer, but his biggest weakness is that he doesn't fully understand the tactical side of the game.

After a disappointing defeat in an important tournament, he meets Tatsuya Fukuda, a coach associated with Tokyo City Esperion FC's youth academy.

Fukuda recognises something special in Ashito and invites him to Tokyo for the academy selection trials.

This is where his real journey begins.

Ashito discovers that being talented in a small local team doesn't automatically make him an excellent player at an elite academy.

He must learn:

  • How to read the entire football pitch.
  • How to position himself correctly.
  • How to understand teammates' movements.
  • How to make decisions before receiving the ball.
  • How to play as part of a tactical system.
  • How to overcome competition and personal weaknesses.
The official story description also highlights Ashito's journey from a raw attacking talent to a player learning the wider tactical dimensions of football.
