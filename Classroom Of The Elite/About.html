Story and Plot:

The story follows Kiyotaka Ayanokoji, a quiet and seemingly ordinary student who enrols at Advanced Nurturing High School, an elite institution designed to prepare students for successful futures.

At first, the school appears to offer complete freedom, excellent facilities, and exceptional opportunities. However, students soon discover that everything is controlled by a strict competitive system.

The school divides students into four classes, from Class A to Class D. Their academic performance, behaviour, teamwork, and results in special examinations determine their progress.

Ayanokoji is placed in Class D, where he meets Suzune Horikita, a highly intelligent student determined to reach Class A.

As the story progresses, students become involved in increasingly complicated competitions involving strategy, teamwork, deception, and psychological battles.

You encounter:

  • Advanced Nurturing High School - an elite institution with a unique education system.
  • Class A, B, C and D - four competing classes with different strengths and ambitions.
  • Special Examinations - challenging tests that require intelligence, teamwork, and strategy.
  • Student Council - an influential organisation within the school.
  • White Room - a mysterious educational programme connected to Ayanokoji's past.
  • Class Leaders - influential students such as Horikita, Ryuuen, Sakayanagi, and Ichinose.
What makes Classroom of the Elite special?
  • An intelligent and mysterious protagonist.
  • Psychological manipulation and mind games.
  • Strategic competitions between students.
  • Unexpected betrayals and plot twists.
  • Complex characters with hidden motivations.
  • A unique school system where intelligence and teamwork determine success.
  • Mystery surrounding Ayanokoji's past and abilities.
The story becomes significantly more intense as Ayanokoji's classmates and rivals begin to realise that the school competition is far more complicated than ordinary academic examinations.
