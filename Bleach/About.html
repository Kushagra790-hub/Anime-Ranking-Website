Story and Plot:

The story follows Ichigo Kurosaki, an ordinary-looking teenager who has an unusual ability: he can see spirits.

His life changes when he encounters Rukia Kuchiki, a Soul Reaper whose responsibility is to protect humans from evil spirits called Hollows and guide souls to the afterlife.

During an encounter with a Hollow, Ichigo receives Soul Reaper powers from Rukia.

This transforms his life completely.

He begins protecting people from supernatural threats while gradually discovering the secrets behind his own identity and abilities.

As the story progresses, the world expands far beyond the human world.

You encounter:

  • Soul Society - the world of Soul Reapers.
  • Hueco Mundo - the world associated with Hollows and Arrancars.
  • The Gotei 13 - the organisation of thirteen Soul Reaper squads.
  • The Arrancars - powerful beings with Hollow and Soul Reaper characteristics.
  • The Quincy - a group with a very different relationship with Hollows.
What makes Bleach special?
  • An interesting supernatural universe.
  • Excellent sword-fighting concepts.
  • Unique character abilities.
  • Powerful transformations.
  • Memorable antagonists.
  • Mystery surrounding Ichigo's origins.
  • Some of the most iconic entrances and character moments in anime.
The story becomes significantly larger as Ichigo discovers that the supernatural world is much more complicated than he initially believed.
