Story and Plot:

The story takes place in an alternate world where the Holy Britannian Empire has become a powerful global superpower.

Britannia conquers Japan and takes away its independence, renaming it Area 11. Japanese citizens are treated as second-class people and are called Elevens.

One day, he encounters a mysterious girl named C.C., who grants him a supernatural ability called Geass.

With this power, Lelouch adopts the identity of Zero and begins a rebellion against Britannia.

However, his objective is not simply to defeat an empire.

He wants to reshape the world according to his own vision of justice.
