Story and Plot:

The story follows Sho Yamato, a young boy who is passionate about mountain biking (MTB) and loves challenging himself through exciting races.

Along with his friends, Kakeru Sakamaki and Makoto Shido, Sho regularly practises at a mountain biking course called the X-Zone.

One day, their group is challenged by a rival mountain biking team called Shark Tooth, led by Gabu Samejima. During their race, a mysterious phenomenon transports Sho and his friends into another world known as the X-Zone.

There, they discover that the only way to return to their original world is by participating in special mountain bike competitions called Idaten Battles and collecting ten golden emblems.

Sho begins an exciting journey across different locations, facing skilled riders, powerful rival teams, and challenging racing courses.

As the story progresses, the adventure becomes much bigger, with Sho discovering new rivals, legendary bikes, and mysteries connected to the X-Zone.

You encounter:

  • X-Zone – A mysterious world where Idaten Battles take place.
  • Idaten Battles – Special mountain bike competitions that test riders' skills, speed, and determination.
  • Flame Kaiser – Sho Yamato's iconic mountain bike, known for its powerful performance.
  • Team Shark Tooth – A rival MTB team led by Gabu Samejima.
  • Neptune – Makoto Shido's Idaten bike.
  • Bloody Fang – Gabu Samejima's powerful mountain bike.
  • Team X – A mysterious and powerful organisation that becomes a major threat.
  • Golden Emblems – Special emblems that Sho must collect to find his way home.
  • Thunder Emperor – Kyoichi Shido's bike, associated with lightning and an eagle, specialising in technical riding.
  • Aero Scissors – Koei's bike, inspired by a fox and recognised for its speed and agility.
  • Hammer Head – Arthur's powerful bike, inspired by a hammerhead shark and designed for strength.
  • Imperial X – A legendary Imperial Bike associated with Takeshi Yamato and extraordinary all-terrain power.
  • Imperial Dragon – Ayumu Yamato's Imperial Bike, known for its dragon-inspired identity.
  • Imperial Tiger – Another Imperial Bike associated with Kyoichi Shido.
What makes Idaten Jump special?
  • Exciting mountain bike racing and competitive battles.
  • Unique bikes with distinctive designs and abilities.
  • Memorable rivalries between MTB riders.
  • Different racing environments, including deserts, volcanoes, snowy mountains, and ruins.
  • Friendship, teamwork, and determination.
  • Interesting bike transformations and special racing techniques.
  • An adventurous storyline filled with mysteries and unexpected challenges.
The story becomes significantly larger as Sho's journey continues beyond the initial races, introducing stronger opponents, new teams, and the mystery surrounding his father's connection to the X-Zone.

What makes Idaten Jump special is how it transforms mountain biking into an adventurous world of competition, friendship, and unforgettable rivalries.
