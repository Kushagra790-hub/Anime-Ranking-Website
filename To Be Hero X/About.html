Story and Plot:

The story takes place in a world where heroes gain their superpowers through people's trust and belief in them.

Unlike traditional superhero stories, being a hero isn't just about having extraordinary abilities. A person's popularity, reputation, and the public's perception determine how powerful they can become.

In this world, people's trust is measured through something called Trust Value. The more people believe in a hero, the stronger that hero becomes. If people lose faith in them, their abilities can weaken.

Every two years, a major tournament is held to determine the rankings of the world's greatest heroes.

At the very top stands the mysterious hero known as X, the number one hero.

As the story progresses, the lives of different heroes begin to connect, revealing a world where fame, public opinion, ambition, and trust are just as important as superpowers.

You encounter:

  • X – The mysterious number one hero who stands at the top of the rankings.
  • Nice – A popular and idealised hero admired by millions.
  • Queen – A powerful and intelligent hero with an extraordinary ability.
  • Dragon Boy – A young hero with tremendous potential.
  • Ghostblade – A mysterious hero known for his distinctive fighting style.
  • The Johnnies – A superhero duo with a unique identity.
  • Lucky Cyan – A hero whose story is closely connected to luck and public belief.
  • E-Soul – A legendary hero with a long history.
  • Ahu – Ahu is a loyal and empathetic dog who becomes a hero after being adopted by Xin Ya.
  • Loli – Loli is a talented young inventor who dreams of becoming a powerful hero.
What makes To Be Hero X special?
  • A unique superhero universe built around public trust.
  • An interesting power system where belief determines strength.
  • Multiple protagonists with their own stories and motivations.
  • Different animation styles and cinematic presentation.
  • Complex themes involving fame, identity, reputation, and society.
  • Unexpected connections between characters and their individual stories.
  • An outstanding soundtrack featuring music by Hiroyuki Sawano.
The story becomes significantly larger as the individual journeys of these heroes connect, gradually revealing that being recognised as a hero and actually being one are two very different things.
